# Market asset logos

These bundled images identify the corresponding securities, cryptocurrencies,
and exchanges. Marks remain the property of their respective owners. Inclusion
does not imply affiliation, endorsement, or a grant of trademark rights.

Downloaded 11 November 2026. PNGs were decoded and resized to at most 160 × 160;
aspect ratios and original colours were preserved. Logos are bundled so showing
an asset does not send user requests to a third-party image service.

pictograms drawn by Kasiva, because a metal has no issuer or company logo.
